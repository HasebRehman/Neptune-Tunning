SERVICE COSTS

What Affects Towing Service Costs?

Towing prices can vary depending on several factors, including:

01

Distance of the tow

Local Tulsa metro tows fall within baseline flat tiers, while longer hauls include straightforward, transparent per-mile rates.

02

Vehicle size and weight

Standard passenger cars require baseline towing equipment, whereas heavy-duty duallys, vans, and commercial trucks require heavier rigs.

03

Type of towing required

Flatbed rollbacks provide 4-wheel off-ground protection for AWDs, EVs, and luxury cars, while wheel-lifts suit tight parking garages.

04

Time of day or emergency needs

Standard business hours run 8 AM – 5 PM; night calls, late weekend hours, and holiday emergencies reflect dedicated on-call staffing.

05

Vehicle condition and accessibility

Missing keys, locked steering wheels, stuck transmissions, or broken ball joints require specialized auxiliary skates to safely load.

06

Recovery or winch-out requirements

Extracting a vehicle stuck in Oklahoma red clay, steep highway ditches, snowdrifts, or muddy terrain involves heavy hydraulic cable winching.

07

Special equipment needed for transport

Low-clearance sports cars, custom modifications, or motorcycles require specialized 4-point soft-straps, wheel chocks, and race ramps.
