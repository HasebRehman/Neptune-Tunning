FAQ

Questions drivers ask us.

Answers to the most common questions about our towing and roadside service in Tulsa — pricing, response time, coverage and more.

How much does towing cost in Tulsa, OK?

Neptune Towing offers transparent pricing. Day rates start at $125 and night rates (after 5:00 PM) start at $195. We always provide a clear quote before beginning any work — no surprise charges. Call (539) 292-3074 for an exact quote.

How fast does Neptune Towing arrive?

We dispatch immediately upon your call. Our drivers are positioned across Tulsa County to minimise response times. In most situations we arrive within 30 to 45 minutes.

Is Neptune Towing available 24 hours a day?

Yes. Neptune Towing provides 24/7 towing and roadside assistance every day of the year, including all holidays. Reach us any hour at (539) 292-3074.

Can Neptune Towing tow motorcycles and luxury vehicles?

Absolutely. We provide specialised motorcycle towing and flatbed towing for luxury, classic, and low-clearance vehicles — transported safely, without damage, regardless of make or value.

What areas does Neptune Towing serve?

Our primary coverage is Tulsa, OK and all of Tulsa County, including Jenks, Bixby, Sand Springs, Broken Arrow, Owasso, Sapulpa, and Glenpool. Call us and our dispatcher will confirm your location right away.
