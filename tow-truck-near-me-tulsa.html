What areas in Tulsa do you cover for wrecker services?

We cover all of Tulsa, including Downtown, Midtown, South Tulsa, and surrounding areas. Whether you’re stuck on I-44, U.S. Route 75, or a quieter road, we can get to you quickly. We also serve areas near major locations like the BOK Center and Tulsa International Airport. No matter where you are in the city, we’re just a call away.

How long does it take for a wrecker to arrive?

In most cases, we aim to reach you within 30 to 45 minutes. Actual time can vary based on traffic, weather, and your exact location. If you’re in a high-traffic area like Downtown Tulsa, it might take a little longer, but we’ll always give you an accurate ETA when you call.

Do you provide wrecker services for large vehicles like RVs and buses?

Absolutely! We offer heavy duty wrecker services for larger vehicles such as RVs, buses, and even commercial trucks. We have specialized equipment to handle vehicles of all sizes, so you can count on us for safe and efficient towing, no matter how big your vehicle is.

What’s the difference between a flatbed and a wheel lift wrecker?

A flatbed wrecker lifts your entire vehicle onto the back of the truck, which is great for luxury cars, motorcycles, and damaged vehicles that shouldn’t be dragged. A wheel lift wrecker, on the other hand, lifts only the front or rear wheels off the ground, making it faster for short-distance tows. Both are safe and effective, but the best choice depends on your vehicle and the situation.

Are there any additional fees for after-hours wrecker service?

Yes, some wrecker services do charge extra for after-hours or emergency calls, especially late at night or on weekends. These fees can range from $50 to $100 depending on the time and circumstances. When you call us, we’ll let you know about any additional charges upfront so that you won’t have any surprises on your bill.
